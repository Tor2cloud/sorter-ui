How it works
  • Each line routes itself by content, not the URL: torrent bytes go to Seedr, everything else fetches straight to FileLu - even a forum attachment link that serves a .torrent.
  • The queue lives here, not in Seedr: as many jobs as fit under 5 GB run together, a job that doesn't fit waits as "held" and starts itself when space frees, and anything over 5 GB alone fails fast.
  • Waiting jobs show an X - tap to cancel. Once a job is moving, it's never interrupted.
  • Movies become "Name (Year)", TV episodes keep their tag - "Show (Year) S02E05". Language picks the folder. Direct links can be any file type: audio, docs, and apps route to their own folders.
  • Done and failed jobs hide under the filter chips. A weekly audit proposes library cleanups - nothing deletes without your approval.
